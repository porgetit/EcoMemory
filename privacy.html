EcoMemory — Privacy & Data Policy

Please read this policy carefully before creating your account. Last updated: June 2026.

1. Data We Collect

When you register and use EcoMemory, we collect the following personal data:

  • Name and email address — used to identify your account.
  • Password — stored with basic security measures to protect access to your account.
  • Uploaded photographs — stored locally on your device within the application's storage directory.
  • Usage metadata — including upload dates, last access timestamps, and erosion levels associated with each file.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Your data is used exclusively to operate the EcoMemory system. Specifically:

  • To authenticate you and maintain your personal session.
  • To apply the progressive digital erosion model to your photographs based on inactivity time.
  • To track and display the erosion state of each file in your gallery.
  • To generate statistical reports within your personal dashboard.
  • To automatically manage the trash cycle (30-day retention before permanent deletion).

3. Storage & Security

All data is stored locally on your device. EcoMemory does not transmit your personal data or photographs to any external server or cloud service.

  • The database is stored in a local SQLite file (data/ecomemory.db).
  • Photographs are stored in the application's local directory (assets/images/).
  • Basic security measures are applied to protect your credentials.
  • No third parties have access to your data.

4. Data Deletion & Erosion Model

EcoMemory's core concept involves the programmed degradation and eventual deletion of inactive files. By using this system, you acknowledge and accept:

  • Photographs that remain inactive beyond the defined threshold will be progressively degraded.
  • Files reaching maximum degradation (100%) are automatically moved to the trash.
  • Files in the trash for more than 30 days are permanently and irreversibly deleted from your device.
  • Viewing a photograph individually resets its erosion counter to zero.
  • Deleted files cannot be recovered once permanently removed.

5. Your Rights & Control

As a user of EcoMemory, you retain full control over your data:

  • You may manually delete any photograph at any time from your gallery or trash.
  • You may restore photographs from the trash before they are permanently deleted.
  • You may view any photograph to reset its erosion counter and preserve it.
  • Since all data is local, you may delete the application and all associated files at any time to remove all stored data.

6. Academic Context

EcoMemory is a functional academic prototype developed as part of a software engineering laboratory project. It is not a commercial product. Its purpose is to validate the technical and conceptual viability of a digital erosion model as a tool to promote conscious data management and reduce digital accumulation.
